Measuring place

Every code the camera reads becomes an occupant with a center point and a width. Distances are measured in code-widths (w), not pixels. So a placement reads the same whether the codes are close to the camera or far from it. Zones are drawn as fractions of the visible frame.

Part 1: Three notions of position

1. Adjacency: “next to”

Condition
Two mice whose centers are less than 2w apart. That leaves less than one code-width of empty space between them.
Metric
d ÷ average code width
Indicator
A dashed white line between the two centers, with a pill showing the distance (e.g. 1.6w).
Behavior (2a)
Bonded pair. If the two mice share 3 or more personality tags, the line turns pink with a ♥. The panel suggests adopting them together.

2. Alignment: “in a row”

Condition
Three or more mice whose centers lie within 0.45w of a single straight line, at any angle. No gap along the line can be bigger than 3w.
Metric
Perpendicular distance from the line, and spacing along it
Indicator
A thick yellow bar through the row and a numbered badge on each mouse.
Behavior (2a)
Adoption line. The row becomes a queue, numbered from the end closest to the Adoption desk. The panel names who is first in line.

3. Zone: “inside”

Condition
A mouse’s center point falls inside a named region of the frame: Nest (bottom left) or Adoption desk (bottom right).
Metric
Point-in-rectangle, with zones set as fractions of the visible frame
Indicator
Zones are always drawn as dashed outlines. When a mouse is inside, the zone fills in, turns solid and shows a count (· 1 inside).
Behavior (2a)
In the Nest, a mouse shows 💤 Napping. At the Adoption desk, a ring fills over 2 seconds and the mouse becomes ✓ Ready to adopt. Leaving the desk resets the timer.

Part 2b: Occupants with abilities

Cat (warn) and Cheese (feed)

Cat
Any mouse within 3w of the cat gets a red dashed line from the cat, a pulsing red ring and a ⚠ Watch out! pill. The site says “Watch out, Jerry!” aloud once each time a mouse comes into range.
Cheese
Any mouse within 2w of the cheese is fed. A yellow bar fills over 4 seconds until the mouse shows 🧀 Full!. Away from cheese, a mouse slowly gets hungry again (about 30 seconds).
